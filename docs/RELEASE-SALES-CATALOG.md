# Sales email, catalogue deletion and product insights

Daily email uses a centred 600px presentation table with inline styles, a primary net-collections card, paid orders / average bill cards, formatted IDR amounts, four summary rows and payment methods. The prior technical dump is removed. Queuing remains outlet-scoped and idempotent; existing sent mail is immutable. No extra report was sent during the redesign.

Overview and Sales reports share inclusive outlet-local From/To dates, today / 7 / 30 day shortcuts and a maximum 366-day span. Top five ranks paid non-voided quantities before refunds, with variants grouped under their parent. Active products with zero sales are shown separately, including zero-stock products. Database aggregation spans the entire selected range. Sales details are paginated beyond the normal 1000-order workspace snapshot. Category filters apply to product rankings and sales detail. The separate existing seven-day chart remains explicitly labelled.

Delete product archives its parent and variants, clears category/photo references and removes them from the catalogue. Original financial and stock records retain foreign keys; future stock restoration remains possible. Archived products cannot be edited or reactivated. Delete category requires zero assigned products; historic category snapshots remain intact and obsolete printer routing is removed. Both endpoints enforce workspace product permissions on the server and audit the action.

Validation: 38 Node tests, syntax checks and static build; rollback-only SQL integration checks exercise variant totals, unsold products, dates, nonempty category denial, deletion with financial history, archived edit denial, stock restoration, foreign workspace denial and email currency/escaping/width. Browser and Gmail-device rendering were not directly tested because the required browser capability is unavailable. Existing Supabase advisory notices are unchanged (private PIN tables, managed pg_net placement and auth password protection setting). No plan or extra emailing quota changes.
